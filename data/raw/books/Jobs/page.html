About No Starch Press

No Starch Press has been publishing books about computers, programming, security, science, and technology since 1994. We're an independent publisher with a reputation for books that are technically serious, carefully edited, and enjoyable to read.

We work closely with our authors, care about the details, and aren't interested in publishing books just to fill a schedule. Our books are distributed worldwide by Penguin Random House, and we have a loyal audience of programmers, hackers, educators, and curious readers.

We're a small, fully remote company. People here have a lot of responsibility, and we expect them to think independently, solve problems, and take initiative.

For full-time positions, applicants must live in one of the following states: CA, GA, IL, MD, ME, MO, NY, OH, OR, WA.

Sales & Business Development Manager

We're looking for someone who can help us find new customers, build relationships, and develop business outside traditional publishing channels.

Our distributor, Penguin Random House, handles sales to bookstores and the usual retail accounts. We're looking for someone to help us find business that doesn't necessarily come through those channels.

That might mean selling books to corporations, training companies, universities, government agencies, conferences, or other organizations. It might mean developing partnerships, custom programs, or opportunities we haven't considered.

We don't expect you to arrive with all the answers. We do expect you to be curious, resourceful, and comfortable figuring things out.

What You'll Do

  • Find and develop new accounts and business opportunities.
  • Identify organizations and markets where our books could sell beyond traditional retail channels.
  • Build relationships with corporations, training companies, universities, schools, government agencies, conferences, professional associations, and specialty retailers.
  • Develop bulk sales, corporate purchases, educational programs, partnerships, and custom publishing opportunities.
  • Build and maintain a sales pipeline, including prospects, contacts, follow-ups, and results.
  • Work with editorial and marketing to identify books, authors, and subjects with additional sales potential.
  • Help develop our foreign-rights business. We can teach you the mechanics.
  • Figure out what's worth pursuing, what isn't, and where to focus your time.

Who We're Looking For

We're less interested in how many years you've worked in publishing than in how you approach problems and opportunities.

  • You're commercially curious. You look at a company or organization and wonder whether there's a way to do business together.
  • You're comfortable prospecting. You can introduce yourself, make calls, write good emails, and follow up.
  • You're persistent. You know that developing an account takes time, and you don't give up after one unanswered email.
  • You're interested in technology. You don't need to be a programmer, but you should be interested in what we publish and willing to learn.
  • You take initiative. You don't wait for someone to hand you a detailed plan.
  • You have good judgment. You can tell the difference between an interesting idea and something likely to produce business.
  • You're organized. You keep track of conversations and follow-ups. Opportunities don't disappear because you forgot about them.
  • You're a good listener. You find out what customers need instead of simply trying to sell them something.
  • You're comfortable discussing money. You can negotiate terms and work through commercial arrangements.
  • You're adaptable. When something doesn't work, you try something else.

Experience in publishing sales, special sales, rights, educational sales, or account management would be useful, but it's not essential. We'd also like to hear from people with backgrounds in technical training, educational technology, conferences, professional associations, technical media, B2B sales, and related businesses.

This isn't a job for someone who wants to inherit a list of accounts, process orders, and follow the traditional publishing sales calendar.

We have a strong publishing program, a recognizable brand, and readers around the world. We think there's a lot of business we're not reaching. We're looking for someone who can help us find it.

Compensation and Benefits

The base salary for this position is $70,000–$85,000, depending on experience. The position is also eligible for performance-based bonuses tied to new business development and sales results.

This is a full-time, remote position with health, dental, and vision insurance, paid time off, and retirement benefits.

To Apply

Send your resume and a short cover letter to [email protected] with "Sales & Business Development Manager" in the subject line.

Tell us why this position interests you and what you've done that's relevant. We'd also like you to tell us about one sales or business development opportunity you think No Starch Press should be pursuing. It doesn't need to be a detailed proposal. We're interested in how you think.

Freelancers

We're always interested in hearing from experienced proofreaders, copyeditors, and indexers. If you'd like to work with us, email [email protected] with a description of your experience and the kinds of projects you've worked on.
